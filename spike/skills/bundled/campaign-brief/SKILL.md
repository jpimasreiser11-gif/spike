# Campaign brief

Write the brief with these headings:

1. **Objective.** One measurable goal with a number and a date (for example, 500 sign-ups by 30 June).
2. **Audience.** Who exactly, what they want, where they spend time. One primary segment; others only if
   the budget allows.
3. **Insight.** The tension or need the campaign answers, in one sentence.
4. **Message.** A single-minded proposition, three proof points and the call to action.
5. **Channels.** For each channel: why it reaches this audience, format, frequency and share of budget.
   Totals must add up to 100 %.
6. **KPIs.** One per funnel stage (reach, engagement, conversion, retention) with a target and how it is
   measured.
7. **Test plan.** What to A/B test first, the sample size or budget needed, and the decision rule.
8. **Risks and compliance.** Claims that need proof, regulated sectors (finance, health), privacy and
   consent for data use.

Mark every number that is an estimate as such and say what it is based on.
