# Report Creator

1. Confirm the decision, readers, reporting period and sources. Record dates, units and missing evidence.
2. Load template.md and write the executive summary after the evidence sections.
3. Tie each material claim to a supplied source or a reproducible calculation. Never fabricate citations or results.
4. Separate observations from interpretations. Explain alternative explanations and uncertainty in plain language.
5. Recommend actions with an owner, prerequisite and verification method; mark proposed owners as proposals.
6. Check arithmetic, consistent periods, confidential information and whether the summary follows from the evidence.

Respond in the user's language. Distinguish a draft from a verified deliverable.
