# SPIKE

> Category: Brand
> SPIKE's own look: a friendly cactus green on cream, deep green ink, rounded shapes and calm, readable type.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

- **Visual style:** friendly, calm, rounded, natural
- **Design intent:** warm and trustworthy; the green is for action and growth, never for decoration everywhere.

## 2. Color

- **Ink:** `#052E1E` — headings, outlines and text on green.
- **Green:** `#88D445` — the primary action and one focal element per view.
- **Green shade:** `#5FBB47` — hover and pressed states of green elements.
- **Mint:** `#E3FADA` — soft highlights, selected rows, badges.
- **Cream:** `#FDFDFA` — page background.
- **Text:** `#13261C` on cream; secondary text `#4B5E52`.
- **Danger:** `#C2412D`; **Warning:** `#A8670B`.

- Put text on green in Ink (`#052E1E`), never in white: white on `#88D445` fails contrast.
- Keep large areas cream or white; green is an accent.

## 3. Typography

- **Families:** display=Nunito (rounded, heavy weights for headings), body=Inter, mono=JetBrains Mono.
- Fonts may not be available offline: always keep the system fallbacks of the tokens.
- Headings 700–900 in Ink; body 400 at 16px with 1.55 line height.

## 4. Spacing & Grid

- **Spacing scale:** 4/8/12/16/24/32/48.
- Generous padding inside cards (16–24px); 14px corner radius on cards, full pills for chips.

## 5. Components

- Buttons: primary is Green with Ink text and a 1px Ink-tinted border; secondary is white with a light border.
- Cards: white surface, 1px border `#E2E8DD`, soft shadow, 14px radius.
- Inputs: white, 1px border, clear focus ring in Green.

## 6. Anti-patterns

- Do not use the cactus logo or SPIKE's name unless the page is about SPIKE.
- Do not use green for body text or long paragraphs.
- Do not add colours outside the palette when a token fits.
