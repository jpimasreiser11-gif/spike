# Brief: first-year report of the Three Threshing Floors Garden

**For:** the neighbours' board of the Three Threshing Floors Association (invented)
**Format:** a report of two pages at most, ready to print
**Tone:** friendly and clear; no jargon; short sentences
**Due:** first week of December

## What we want to achieve

In December the board decides whether to grow the garden with a second plot. It needs a report that says, without
frills, what happened in the first year, what we learned and what the team proposes.

## Requested structure

1. **Results.** Three or four key figures in a small table and a paragraph that explains them.
2. **Lessons learned.** Three lessons, each with a concrete example from the year.
3. **Next steps.** One clear recommendation (expand, wait or expand halfway) and the three most urgent tasks if it
   is approved.

## Rules

- Use only the figures in `data.md`. If a figure is missing, write "figure pending"; do not invent it.
- Every figure carries its unit (kilos, euros, people).
- A headline at the top that sums up the report in one sentence.
- Add one sentence of thanks to the volunteers, without naming anyone.

## What you can ask Creator

- "Write the report following the brief."
- "Make it a Word document" or "prepare a one-page version for the notice board."
- "Suggest a simple chart of the harvest by quarter."
