## Team notes

- Tomatoes and pumpkins were the most productive crops; the summer lettuces spoiled in the July heat.
- In August there was no water for two weeks because the pump broke; it was fixed with a contribution of 85 EUR.
- There is a waiting list of 11 families to join the garden.
- The tool shed became too small in autumn.
- We do not yet have the figure for total volunteer working hours.
